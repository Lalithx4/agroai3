    One feature: your plant calls you back

    Scan→seconds later it rings→"Tomato, east field, calling"→hands-free voice talk→call log + SMS summary→follow-up photo

    The shape

    Role-play ideas (follow-up, recovery check) give it purpose. What-if ideas (plant as caller, memory, mood voice) give it soul. Reverse-brainstorm safeguards (opt-in, quiet hours, confidence guard on calls) make it credible. The conversation reuses the existing PlantVoice loop.

    Two phases

    Phase 1, hackathon, no telephony account: in-app incoming-call UI, ringtone, vibration and a background Notification, triggered by "Call me in 30 s" on the report and by a rules trigger engine. Runs on Sarvam STT/TTS + Bedrock/Sarvam chat already in the code.
    Phase 2, after: real PSTN calls via Exotel (India) or Twilio with streaming audio to Sarvam, which also reaches feature-phone farmers. That is the inclusion story.

    Hidden links

    "Judges think it's pre-recorded" is solved by the live-language idea: let a judge pick Telugu or Hindi and ask anything mid-call. "Feels robotic" latency is solved by streaming TTS plus barge-in, which also delivers the ask that it talk like a person.

    DecisionBuild Phase 1, "Plant calls you back": incoming-call screen, call conversation, call log and follow-up reminder inside CropMagix. Document Phase 2 Exotel/Twilio real calls as the roadmap.
